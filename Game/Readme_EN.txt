"Eat the bullets!"

Method of operation:
Arrow keys: airplane direction
Z: Shooting
Left Shift: low speed
R: restart (when game over)
Esc: exit the game

Mechanics Description:
When you level up, you can eat some of the bullets
There are five levels from 0 to 4
Yellow bullets: you can eat them when you reach level 1
Orange bullets: You can eat them when you reach level 2
Red bullets: you can eat them when you reach level 3
Purple bullets: you can eat them when you reach level 4
Black and white bullets: can't be eaten, must be avoided
Eating a green prop will give you +10 energy, eating a bullet will give you +2 energy, and your level will increase when energy reach 100.
After being shot, life-1, energy-20, if energy is not enough, you will lower your level.

Score system:
Bullet hits an enemy +5 points, kills an enemy +200 points.
Eat a green prop +50 points, eat a bullet +20 points.
Life +1 when score reaches (2500*(2^n),n>=0).
